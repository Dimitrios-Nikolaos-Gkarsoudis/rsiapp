## 3. What we process and why

### Location

- **When:** only after you agree to share your location, either during first launch or later from the location button, and your phone's permission is granted.
- **While you use the app:** your location is used to show your position on the map, centre the map on you, plan routes from where you are and rank nearby search results.
- **During navigation:** while turn-by-turn navigation is active, the app keeps running with an ongoing notification and may continue to use your location when the screen is off or you switch to another app. This is used to follow your route and warn you about risky road segments ahead.
- **Where it goes:** your location is processed on your phone. It is also included in place searches and route requests, as described below. On Android, location is provided by your phone's location services, which may include Google Play services.
- **Legal basis:** your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw consent at any time in your phone's settings. This does not affect processing that took place before you withdrew it.

### Place search

- **What:** the text you type in the search box, and your current position when location is shared, so nearby results appear first.
- **Where it goes:** sent to Mapbox, which returns matching places.
- **Legal basis:** providing the feature you ask for (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR). Using your position to rank results relies on your consent to share location.

### Routes

- **What:** the coordinates of your starting point and destination. When you use "Your location" as the start, this includes your current position.
- **Where it goes:** sent to our routing service provider, which calculates the route. The app currently uses the public OSRM routing service at router.project-osrm.org. [Confirm or replace the routing provider before release.]
- **Legal basis:** providing the feature you ask for (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

### Maps

- **What:** to display the map, the app downloads map data from Mapbox. Like any online service, Mapbox receives technical information such as your IP address and device and app details.
- **Mapbox telemetry:** the Mapbox map software may send usage information to Mapbox and, where location permission is granted, location information used to improve Mapbox's maps, as described in Mapbox's privacy policy. Where available, you can switch this off from the ⓘ button on the map.
- **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in providing a working map in the app (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

### Settings stored on your phone

- **What:** whether you have completed the introduction and whether you chose to share your location.
- **Where it stays:** only on your phone. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
- **Legal basis:** providing the app as you set it up (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

### Accident data, road risk and filters

- Accident information, road risk levels and rental listings are included with the app. Viewing them, tapping on them or changing map filters happens on your phone and does not send personal data to us.

### Car rental listings

- The app shows listings and offers from rental companies. We do not share your personal data with rental companies.
- If you contact a rental company using the phone number, email address or website shown, you share information directly with that company. It handles your data under its own privacy policy.

### Safety alerts and notifications

- Audio safety alerts and the ongoing navigation notification are created on your phone. They do not send data to us.

## 4. What we do not do

## 8. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

- access the personal data held about you;
- have inaccurate data corrected;
- have your data erased;
- restrict or object to certain processing;
- receive your data in a portable format;
- withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing.

Because we do not keep your location, searches or routes on our own servers, most data can be controlled directly on your phone: change location permission in your settings, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. For requests about data held by our service providers, you can contact them directly or ask us for help.

To exercise your rights, contact us at [contact email]. We will reply within one month, as required by law.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Greece this is the **Hellenic Data Protection Authority** (https://www.dpa.gr). You can also complain to the authority in the EU country where you live or work.

## 9. Managing location access

- **Choose during first launch:** select "Use my location" or "Not now".
- **Turn it on later:** tap the location button on the map.
- **Turn it off:** go to your phone's settings, find Road Safety Insights and change its location permission. You can also stop navigation to end background use during a trip.

## 10. Children

The app is not intended for children under 16, and we do not knowingly process personal data of children under 16. If you believe a child has used the app and shared personal data, contact us and we will help.

## 11. Security

Requests from the app to our service providers are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Settings on your phone are protected by your phone's operating system. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.
